# TerraQuest AI — Hackathon Pitch & Live Demo Script 🌿
## "Touch Grass, Not Screens: Your Offline AI Companion for the Real World"

**Target Presentation Time:** 3 - 5 Minutes

---

### Slide 1: The Problem (0:00 - 0:45)
> *"Judges, let's look around: society is suffering from profound nature deficit disorder. The average person spends over 7 hours a day staring at glowing screens. Even when people want to explore nature, existing AI assistants are designed with one perverse incentive: to keep you trapped in the app chatting forever.*
>
> *We asked ourselves a simple question:*
> **What if an AI's core metric of success was how quickly it helped you put your phone away?**
>
> *Introducing **TerraQuest AI** — an offline, privacy-first open-source nature companion built for the Touch Grass Open-Source AI Challenge."*

---

### Slide 2: The Solution & Live Walkthrough (0:45 - 2:30)

#### 1. Daily Mission & The Signature "Explore, Then Disconnect" Flow
> *"Let's open the app. Right on the dashboard, TerraQuest AI presents today's outdoor quest: **'The 20-Minute Sensory Nature Immersion'**.
> Instead of keeping us inside, notice what happens when we click **'Launch Pocket Mode'**..."*
>
> *(Demo action: Click 'Pocket Mode')*
>
> *"Boom. The entire UI transforms into an ultra-clean, OLED-friendly, outdoor field card. It shows you what to pack, gives you your immediate step, starts a gentle exploration timer, and explicitly instructs you: **'Lock your screen and step into the woods.'**"*

#### 2. The Return & Mindful Reflection
> *"Now imagine we've spent 20 minutes in our local park. We return home, open the app, and tap **'I Have Returned — Record Discoveries'**."*
>
> *(Demo action: Click 'I Have Returned')*
>
> *"We note what we heard: 'Two American Robins singing at dawn, crisp pine scent, gentle wind.' The local open-weight AI synthesizes our observations into an encouraging naturalist reflection, awards us +65 XP, unlocks a milestone badge, and automatically saves the entry to our private, offline field journal."*

#### 3. Species Identification with Honest Offline Intelligence
> *"Let's visit the **Identify** workspace. We support camera capture, image uploads, and an innovative **Guided Naturalist Wizard** that works even when you don't have a camera or vision model!*
>
> *(Demo action: Navigate to /identify -> Guided Morphology)*
>
> *"We select 'Bird', notice 'Red/Orange breast', pick 'Cone beak', and hit identify. Our local heuristic engine matches it against the local taxonomy database: **American Robin** (88% match). Crucially, we clearly highlight:
> - Distinguishing field marks
> - Habitat & independent tips
> - **Field Safety Notices & Strict Edible Warnings** (e.g. for poison ivy and giant hogweed). We never hallucinate safety."*

#### 4. Open-Source Audio Recognition Boundary
> *"Notice the **Bird-Call Audio** tab: we built a working Web Audio recording pipeline with live real-time spectrogram visualization, providing an honest, reproducible integration boundary for open-weight audio models like BirdNET-Lite."*

#### 5. OpenStreetMap & Adventure Planner
> *(Demo action: Navigate to /adventure)*
> *"Our Smart Adventure Planner generates customizable 15-to-60 minute loops tailored to terrain accessibility and nature interests, rendered on open-source OpenStreetMap with offline tile caching."*

### Slide 3: Why Open Innovation Matters (2:30 - 3:30)
> *"Why did we choose an open-source first architecture?*
> 1. **Privacy:** Your nature journals, walking coordinates, and personal reflections belong to you. With IndexedDB, your data never leaves your device.
> 2. **Offline Resilience:** Nature trails and mountain forests often have zero cellular signal. With Ollama and our local taxonomy heuristics, TerraQuest AI works 100% offline in deep backcountry.
> 3. **Zero Operating Costs:** No $20/month subscriptions or per-token charges. You can run Qwen 2.5, Gemma 2, or LLaVA locally without gatekeepers.
> 4. **Model Agility:** Under the hood, our clean `AIProvider` interface lets users swap models in one click under Settings without touching code."*
